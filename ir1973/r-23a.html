Rule 23a – Prior application as state of the artRegel 23a – Frühere Anmeldung als Stand der TechnikRègle 23a – Demande antérieure en tant qu'état de la techniqueZasada 23a – Wcześniejsze zgłoszenie jako stan techniki

A European patent application shall be considered as comprised in the state of the art under Article 54, paragraphs 3 and 4, only if the designation fees under Article 79, paragraph 2, have been validly paid.
