Paris Convention

Art. 22 – Consequences of Ratification or AccessionArt. 22 – Consequences of Ratification or AccessionArt. 22 – Effet de la ratification ou de l’adhésionArt. 22 – Consequences of Ratification or Accession

No text in this language for the chosen date.

Subject to the possibilities of exceptions provided for in Articles 20(1)(b) and 28(2), ratification or accession shall automatically entail acceptance of all the clauses and admission to all the advantages of this Act.
