Rule 65 – Transmittal of the European search reportRegel 65 – Übermittlung des europäischen RecherchenberichtsRègle 65 – Transmission du rapport de recherche européenneZasada 65 – Przekazanie sprawozdania z poszukiwania europejskiego

No text in this language for the chosen date.

Immediately after it has been drawn up, the European search report shall be transmitted to the applicant. The European Patent Office shall make available copies of any cited documents.
