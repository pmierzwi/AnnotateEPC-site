(1) The European Patent Office shall decide in what form the opinion of an expert whom it appoints shall be submitted.
1 connection
(2) The terms of reference of the expert shall include:
(a) a precise description of the task;
1 connection
(b) the period specified for the submission of the opinion;
(c) the names of the parties to the proceedings;
(d) particulars of the rights which the expert may invoke under Rule 122, paragraphs 2 to 4.
1 connection
(3) A copy of any written opinion shall be submitted to the parties.
1 connection
(4) The parties may object to an expert. The department of the European Patent Office concerned shall decide on the objection.

1 connection

1 connection

(4) Die Beteiligten können den Sachverständigen ablehnen. Über die Ablehnung entscheidet das Organ des Europäischen Patentamts, das für die Beauftragung des Sachverständigen zuständig ist.
