Art. 38 – The Organisation's own resourcesArt. 38 – Eigene Mittel der OrganisationArt. 38 – Ressources propres de l'OrganisationArt. 38 – Środki własne Organizacji

No text in this language for the chosen date.

The Organisation's own resources shall be the yield from the fees laid down in this Convention, and also all receipts, whatever their nature.
2 connections

Source: EPC 1973 (2006 edition)

The Organisation's own resources shall comprise:
2 connections
(a) all income from fees and other sources and also the reserves of the Organisation;
(b) the resources of the Pension Reserve Fund, which shall be treated as a special class of asset of the Organisation, designed to support the Organisation's pension scheme by providing the appropriate reserves.
