Art. 71 – Transfer and constitution of rightsArt. 71 – Übertragung und Bestellung von RechtenArt. 71 – Transfert et constitution de droitsArt. 71 – Przeniesienie i powstawanie praw

A European patent application may be transferred or give rise to rights for one or more of the designated Contracting States.
