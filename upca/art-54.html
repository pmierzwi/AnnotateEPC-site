Art. 54 – Burden of proofArt. 54 – BeweislastArt. 54 – Charge de la preuveArt. 54 – Burden of proof

No text in this language for the chosen date.

Without prejudice to Article 24(2) and (3), the burden of the proof of facts shall be on the party relying on those facts.
