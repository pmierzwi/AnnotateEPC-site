Art. 98 – Publication of the specification of the European patentArt. 98 – Veröffentlichung der europäischen PatentschriftArt. 98 – Publication du fascicule du brevet européenArt. 98 – Publikacja opisu patentu europejskiego

No text in this language for the chosen date.

The European Patent Office shall publish the specification of the European patent as soon as possible after the mention of the grant of the European patent has been published in the European Patent Bulletin.
