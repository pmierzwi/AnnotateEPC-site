EPC 2000

Art. 83 – Disclosure of the inventionArt. 83 – Offenbarung der ErfindungArt. 83 – Exposé de l'inventionArt. 83 – Ujawnienie wynalazku

No text in this language for the chosen date.

The European patent application shall disclose the invention in a manner sufficiently clear and complete for it to be carried out by a person skilled in the art.
